Design, development and digital creativity

I'm a Front-End Developer and Digital Designer with more than 8 years of experience creating projects for companies, brands and professionals across a wide range of industries.

My work combines development, design and creativity — from web layout and interface design to Branding, app prototyping, print design and image vectorization.

Throughout my career, I have worked on more than 44 eCommerce projects and designed and prototyped more than 20 applications, collaborating with companies, Instagram influencers, YouTubers, small businesses and individual clients.

I also have more than 7 years of experience creating visual content and marketing campaigns, developing creative assets designed not only to communicate a strong visual identity, but also to achieve real results.

I like to get involved in every project, understand what the client actually needs and find solutions that combine functionality, design and personality. Curiosity, adaptability and a constant desire to learn are an essential part of the way I work.
